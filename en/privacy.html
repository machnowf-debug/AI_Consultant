3. Hosting and server log files

This website is hosted by Netlify, Inc., 512 2nd Street, Suite 200, San Francisco, CA 94107, USA.

When you access the site, the hosting provider automatically collects information transmitted by your browser and stores it in server log files, in particular your IP address, date and time of access, the file requested and the volume of data transferred, the browser and operating system used, and the referrer URL.

This data is required for the technical delivery of the site and for defending against attacks. The legal basis is Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR; my legitimate interest lies in the secure and reliable operation of the website. This data is not merged with other data sources and is not evaluated for marketing purposes.

A data processing agreement pursuant to Art. 28 GDPR is in place with Netlify. As personal data may also be transferred to the USA, such transfers are based on the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses pursuant to Art. 46 (2) (c) GDPR and, where applicable, on the provider's certification under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework pursuant to Art. 45 GDPR.

4. Appointment booking via Cal.com

For scheduling intro calls I use Cal.com, provided by Cal.com, Inc., 2093 Philadelphia Pike #3427, Claymont, DE 19703, USA.

The calendar does not load automatically. You initially see only a notice. A connection to Cal.com servers is established only when you actively click “Load calendar and pick a slot”. In doing so, your IP address, information about your browser and device and the page requested are transmitted to Cal.com. Cal.com may set cookies or comparable technologies on your device in this context.

If you subsequently book an appointment, I process the details you provide, typically name, email address, the requested slot and voluntary information such as your company or a short description of your request. I use this data solely to conduct and prepare the call.

The legal basis for loading the calendar and the associated access to your device is your consent pursuant to Art. 6 (1) (a) GDPR and Section 25 (1) TDDDG. The legal basis for processing the booking data is Art. 6 (1) (b) GDPR, as processing serves pre-contractual measures. You may withdraw your consent at any time with effect for the future, for example via the “Withdraw consent” link in the footer.

Data may also be transferred to the USA by Cal.com. Such transfers are based on the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses pursuant to Art. 46 (2) (c) GDPR. A data processing agreement pursuant to Art. 28 GDPR is in place. Further information is available in the provider's privacy policy at cal.com/privacy.

5. Storing your consent in the browser

So that you do not have to grant consent for the calendar on every visit, your decision is stored locally in your browser's local storage under the key fm-cal-consent-v1. Only the fact that consent has been given is stored. There is no link to your person, no profiling and no transfer to third parties. You can delete this entry at any time by clicking “Withdraw consent” in the footer or by clearing site data in your browser.

6. Contact by email

If you write to me by email, I process your details in order to handle your enquiry and any follow-up questions. The legal basis is Art. 6 (1) (b) GDPR where your enquiry relates to entering into or performing a contract, otherwise Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR based on my legitimate interest in responding to enquiries. Enquiries are deleted once they are no longer required, at the latest after statutory retention periods expire.
